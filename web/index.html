Classification guide

Scope signals—not confirmed root causes.

  • dependency-wide: declared dependency and service failures overlap at quorum.
  • likely service-wide: service failures meet the site quorum.
  • site-local/connectivity-path: one failed site is corroborated by healthy sites.
  • insufficient evidence/unknown: coverage or pattern is inconclusive.

Correlated dependency failures do not prove causation. Missing and unknown checks remain explicit.

Privacy limit

A schema pass is not a safe-to-share certification.

The capsule schema rejects known raw-data field names and keeps a replay recipe synthetic. It cannot detect sensitive details hidden in free text or disguised under another key. Manually review timestamps, categories, versions, and descriptions before sharing.

Never place original traces, waveforms, credentials, or person/device identifiers in a capsule.
